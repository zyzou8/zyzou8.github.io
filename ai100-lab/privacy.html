AI 100 Tutor Bridge: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

AI 100 Tutor Bridge is a browser extension for the AI 100 Debugging Lab course page (zyzou8.github.io/ai100-lab). It passes a student's question from that page to the student's own Penn State AI Studio chat, and passes the reply back to the page.

What the extension handles

  • The question you send: the task, your code, and the answers you type on the course page.
  • The tutor's reply: the text AI Studio writes back.

Where that data goes

  • Your question is typed into your own Penn State AI Studio chat (aistudio.psu.edu), in the browser session you are already signed in to. AI Studio's own privacy terms apply there.
  • The reply is shown on the course page in your browser.
  • The extension does not send any data to the developer or to any other server, and it has no server of its own.

What the extension does not do

  • It never reads or stores passwords, sign-in tokens or cookies.
  • It only runs on aistudio.psu.edu and the course page. It does not read other websites or your browsing history.
  • It does not sell, share or use data for advertising, credit or any purpose other than passing questions and replies.

Course page

The course page keeps a short activity log (questions, hint levels, difficulty ratings) in your browser only. It is erased when you reload or close the page. If the course later collects this data for research, students will be told first and asked for consent.
